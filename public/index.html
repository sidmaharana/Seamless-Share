What is ShareKaro?

ShareKaro is a secure, privacy-first file and text sharing platform designed for instant, encrypted transfers. Whether you need to share sensitive documents, code snippets, or temporary messages, ShareKaro provides a fast, reliable, and completely anonymous solution without requiring registration or account creation.

🔒 End-to-End Encrypted

All files and text are encrypted with AES-256 before leaving your device. Only the recipient can decrypt your data.

⚡ Lightning Fast

Optimized infrastructure ensures rapid uploads and downloads. Share files in seconds, not minutes.

🗑️ Auto-Delete

Files automatically expire after 5 minutes, text after 10 minutes. Your data never lingers on our servers.

    🔐 Security & Privacy
    
        
    
          
    
            
    Military-Grade Encryption
    
            
    ShareKaro implements AES-256 encryption, the same standard used by governments and financial institutions worldwide. Your data is encrypted on your device before it ever reaches our servers.
    
          
    
          
    
            
    Zero-Knowledge Architecture
    
            
    We operate on a zero-knowledge principle, meaning we cannot access your files even if we wanted to. Only you and the recipient have the encryption keys.
    
          
    
          
    
            
    No Data Collection
    
            
    We don't collect personal information, track users, or sell data. Your privacy is not a feature—it's our foundation.
    
          
    
          
    
            
    Automatic Expiration
    
            
    Files and text automatically delete from our servers after their expiration period. We don't retain your data longer than necessary.
